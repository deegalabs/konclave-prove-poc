Proving PoC

One 2-action proof of the orchard 0.15.5 Action circuit, WebAssembly, single thread, synthetic data. No vault, no network after load, no analytics, nothing leaves this page.

Expect from ~40 s on a recent phone to 5+ minutes on a low-end one, and a warm device. First the proving key is built (once per page load), then each run. Stop kills the worker.

(actions = max(2, outputs); 1 is the default 2-action proof)
